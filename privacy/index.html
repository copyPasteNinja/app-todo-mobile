ProWeek Privacy Policy

Updated November 5, 2026

ProWeek is a weekly planner for iPhone and Mac. It was built so your plans stay yours: ProWeek does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

What ProWeek collects

Nothing. ProWeek has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that tracks you. The app never sends your information to us or to anyone else. Its only network connection is optional iCloud sync, described below, which is off unless you turn it on.

Where your data lives

The tasks, priorities and settings you enter are stored on your device:

  • In the app. Your tasks are kept in a database inside ProWeek's storage on your device. The widget reads the same data on your device.
  • Daily backups. ProWeek saves a copy of your tasks on your device (on iPhone, in Files › On My iPhone › ProWeek › Backups; on Mac, open ProWeek's Settings and choose Show backups in Finder). These files stay on your device unless you choose to move or share them.
  • Device backups. If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, Apple may include ProWeek's data in that backup. Those backups are managed by Apple under your settings and Apple's privacy policy.

Because we never receive your data, we cannot see, recover or delete it for you. You can delete it at any time by deleting tasks in the app, removing backup files, or deleting ProWeek from your device.

Optional iCloud sync

ProWeek can sync your tasks between your iPhone and Mac using iCloud. It is off by default, and you can turn it on or off in ProWeek's Settings.

  • When it's on, your tasks are stored in a private iCloud database that belongs to your Apple Account. ProWeek uses the Apple Account you're already signed in with; there is no separate ProWeek account.
  • We, the developers, cannot see the contents of your private iCloud database. Apple stores it under Apple's privacy policy, encrypted in transit and on Apple's servers. If you turn on Advanced Data Protection, Apple applies end-to-end encryption to more of your iCloud data.
  • Turning sync off stops syncing on that device. To remove the synced copy, manage ProWeek's data in your device's iCloud settings.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, ProWeek schedules them on your device with Apple's notification system. Reminders are created on your device and are not sent through any server.

Children

ProWeek collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If ProWeek ever starts collecting data, this page will say exactly what and why before that version is released.

ProWeek Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

ProWeek is a weekly planner for iPhone. It was built so your plans stay yours: ProWeek does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

What ProWeek collects

Nothing. ProWeek has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that tracks you. The app does not connect to the internet and does not send your information to us or to anyone else.

Where your data lives

The tasks, priorities and settings you enter are stored only on your iPhone:

  • In the app. Your tasks are kept in a database inside ProWeek's storage on your device. The Home Screen widget reads the same data on your device.
  • Daily backups. ProWeek saves a copy of your tasks to Files › On My iPhone › ProWeek › Backups. These files stay on your device unless you choose to move or share them.
  • Device backups. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, Apple may include ProWeek's data in that backup. Those backups are managed by Apple under your settings and Apple's privacy policy.

Because we never receive your data, we cannot see, recover or delete it for you. You can delete it at any time by deleting tasks in the app, removing backup files in the Files app, or deleting ProWeek from your iPhone.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, ProWeek schedules them on your iPhone with Apple's notification system. Reminders are created on your device and are not sent through any server.

Children

ProWeek collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If ProWeek ever starts collecting data, this page will say exactly what and why before that version is released.

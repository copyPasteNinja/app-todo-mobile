Plan once. Then just do today.

Three steps, every week. No projects, no tags, no due times.

1

Write it all down

At the start of your week, add everything on your mind. Give each task one number: how much it matters, from 1 to 10.

2

Spread it across the week

One tap lays your tasks out evenly over your work days, with the highest priorities on the earliest days.

3

Just do today

Each morning, open ProWeek and see only today's list, sorted by what matters most. Check things off and move on.

Life happens. Your plan keeps up.

ProWeek handles the reshuffling, so you don't have to.

  • New task mid-week? It lands on your lightest remaining day.
  • Didn't finish something? It moves to your next work day on its own.
  • Plans changed? Re-plan the rest of the week in one tap.

A fresh start every week.

Pick the day your week resets. Anything left over comes back for a quick review.

  • Keep it, with a new priority if things have changed.
  • Or let it go, and start the week clean.

See how far you've come.

Overview shows your week at a glance, without turning it into a spreadsheet.

  • How much of the week is done, and how each day went.
  • Your streak of fully finished days.
  • Look back at any past week.

Thoughtful details.

Small things that make it pleasant to come back to every day.

Home Screen widget

Light and dark

Daily backups

Made for the Mac, too.

Not a stretched phone app. ProWeek on the Mac is built for the keyboard, with a menu item and a shortcut for everything.

⌘N

Quick add

Add a task from anywhere in the app. Type !8 to give it priority 8.

↑↓space

Today from the keyboard

Move through the list and check things off. Number keys set priority.

⇧⌘S

Spread the week

Lay out everything in one stroke, then drag tasks between days on the week board.
